Setup checklist

  1. Activate email delivery

    Place one test order on the website. FormSubmit will email an activation link. Click Activate Form; every order after that arrives in your inbox.

  2. Change the admin passcode

    Go to and set your own passcode, then publish.

  3. Check prices & delivery charges

    Review and the delivery fees in Settings.

  4. Publish

    Download data.js from and upload it to your site.

How to publish your changes

  1. Preview your changes.
  2. Download the updated data file.
  3. Upload it to your site, replacing assets/js/data.js. On GitHub: open the repository → assets/js → Add file → Upload files → drop in data.js → Commit changes. The site updates within a minute or two.
