FAQ

Questions, answered

What does “topical” mean?

Instead of being sorted by year, every past paper question is grouped by syllabus topic. You can practise all the Electricity questions together, then all the Waves questions, and so on.

Which years are covered?

Our topical books include questions from 2016 to 2026.

How much is delivery?

Delivery charges depend on your location and are shown at checkout before you place the order.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery: pay the delivery rider when your order arrives.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 2–3 business days after we confirm your order.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes. Call or message us before your order is dispatched and we'll update it.
